The opportunity

Your people shouldn't spend their time doing work technology can do better.

Businesses are full of processes that have evolved over years: spreadsheets, manual reporting, copying data between systems, email-driven workflows, reconciliations, checks and approvals.

Featured use case · Financial services

Stop producing information manually. Start using it intelligently.

A major investment bank maintained a catalogue of manually generated management information reports. Teams were required to extract data from upstream systems, manipulate datasets, perform calculations, format outputs and distribute reports to multiple stakeholders.

A fully automated reporting model can ingest upstream data, perform transformations and calculations, apply validation and control checks, dynamically produce the required reports and distribute them to the appropriate recipients — alongside a real-time or near-real-time interactive dashboard.

Less manual effort Reduce time spent preparing and formatting reports.
Greater consistency Standardise calculations, business rules and outputs.
Stronger controls Embed validation, traceability and exception handling.
More valuable capacity Move teams from production to analysis and decision-making.
